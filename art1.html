Aurora Bloom

by Lena Wren

Artist
Lena Wren
Year
2023
Medium
Digital Painting

Lena Wren built Aurora Bloom from three overlapping ribbons of colour, each one meant to catch light the way charged particles do above the polar circle. The palette moves from mint through violet without ever settling, so the piece reads differently depending on the hour you view it. Below the glow, a dark ridge of mountains holds the composition still — a quiet floor beneath all that movement.
