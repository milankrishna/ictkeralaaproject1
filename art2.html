Quiet Landscape

Silent Forest

by Mateus Cole

Artist
Mateus Cole
Year
2021
Medium
Digital Illustration

Mateus Cole stacked Silent Forest from four separate tree lines, each one darker and closer than the last, with two thin bands of fog pulled between them to hold the depth apart. A pale sun sits low in the far layer, doing very little except proving the whole scene has a light source. Cole has said the piece is less about any single tree and more about the quiet math of standing far back from a lot of them at once.
